## Rules

- Keep model names, dataset names, library names, conference names, and acronyms in their canonical form.
- On first occurrence, use Chinese plus the established acronym when helpful.
- Do not translate “expert” as a government ministry or “router” as a networking device when the context is MoE.
- Preserve mathematical operators such as softmax, argmax, LayerNorm, ReLU, and GELU.
- Prefer “本文” for “this paper” and “我们” for authorial “we” unless the venue style suggests otherwise.
